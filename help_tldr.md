# [04] USER OPERATION QUICK START (TL;DR)

A short, straightforward guide on how to control the Toolkit.

### 1. Spoofing your MAC
* Pick your card, choose a profile or select `[Enter Custom]`.
* Click `Rand` for a safe address, click `Apply`, and enter your root password.
* Click `Default for interface` to restore your hardware-burned MAC.

### 2. Running a Scan
* Open the scanner tab, hit `↻`, and wait 1 second.
* Double-click any empty `Name` cell to link a custom alias, then click `Save to Config`.
* Click `MacChange` on any row to instantly copy its MAC parameters to the spoofer.

### 3. Monitoring Nodes
* Open the ping tab, press `+` to open isolated tracking tabs.
* Click `Start Ping`. Green is healthy, Red is connection dropped.
